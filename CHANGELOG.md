# Changelog

All notable changes to LookOut are in this file. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-29

### Added

- Countdown from the last break, with a full-screen break screen on every
  monitor.
- Bar pill with the time to the next break, and a popup to start, postpone,
  or pause a break.
- Time without input, and sleep, count as a break.
- A break that comes due during a call or a playing video waits until it
  ends.
- Settings in the popup: interval, break length, idle time, wait for calls
  and videos, allow skipping, apps that are not calls.
- `omarchy-shell lookout` commands: start, skip, postpone, pause, resume,
  toggle, status.
